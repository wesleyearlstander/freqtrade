## Aggressive Enhancement Plan

### 🚀 **Phase 1: Ultra-Aggressive Position Sizing**

- **Current**: Max 180% position size in perfect conditions
- **Enhanced**: Up to 400% position size when:
  - Multiple trend confirmations align
  - Low portfolio heat (<20%)
  - Strong momentum signals
  - Recent winning streak

### ⚡ **Phase 2: Maximum Leverage Utilization**

- **Current**: Max 3x leverage in perfect conditions
- **Enhanced**: Up to 5x leverage when:
  - Extremely strong trending conditions
  - Multiple timeframe alignment
  - High confidence signals
  - Portfolio heat very low

### 📈 **Phase 3: Pyramiding System**

- Add to winning positions as they move in our favor
- Scale in with 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% position sizes
- Use trailing stops to protect profits
- Compound gains within single trades

### 🎯 **Phase 4: Signal Amplification**

- Lower entry thresholds for trending markets
- Add momentum oscillators (RSI, MACD)
- Include volume confirmation
- Multi-timeframe trend alignment

### 🔥 **Phase 5: Compound Growth Acceleration**

- Reinvest all profits immediately
- Scale position sizes with account growth
- Use Kelly Criterion for optimal sizing
- Implement winning streak amplification
